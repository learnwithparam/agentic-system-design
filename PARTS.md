# Parts

Every part and step of the course, for the sessions that build parts 2 to 4. A part session starts from this
file alone, plus `STANDARD.md` and the conventions below.

## Conventions every step follows

- **One doc, built up.** The learner writes one `design.md`. Each step's `problem/design.md` holds the
  sections from the steps before it (copied from their solutions) plus the empty section for this step.
  `solution/design.md` is the same with this step's section filled. Later deliverables sit next to it in the
  step folder: `cost.yaml`, `adr/NNNN-name.md`, `review-<case>.md`, `review-request.md`.
- **A section** is a `## Name` heading with prose, then one fenced block tagged `yaml design`. Read it with
  `data(dir, "Name")` from `checks/lib/design.ts`. Starters list the job ids with empty fields.
- **The world** a check may read: `world/desk.yaml` (jobs, hard rules, volumes; `readDesk()` in
  `checks/lib/world.ts`), the tables of a freshly seeded OMS (`recordTables()`), and the finished system's
  board over three fresh replay runs (`finishedBoards(3)`). A check never reads a model's reply.
- **Pricing** uses `checks/lib/cost.ts` with the prices in `config/models.ts` (Anthropic list prices, source and read date in that file). **ADRs** use `checks/lib/adr.ts` (MADR 4.0.0, Confirmation required).
- **Ladder and patterns** are defined once in `checks/lib/ladder.ts`.
- Every rule's `tryNext` is a sentence. Every step has at least one `wrong/<mistake>/` that fails exactly the
  rule it is named for. A step is 5 to 30 minutes and its README stays under 600 words.
- When a part gets its steps, delete its `Status: planned.` line and replace each `TODO(part session)`.
