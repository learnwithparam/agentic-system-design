# Copperbeck Power storm desk policy

These are the desk's written rules. Every design you write for Copperbeck must respect them.

## P1. Switching

Only an authorised switching engineer may open or close a switch, recloser or fuse. The engineer reads the
switching plan, confirms the line is safe, and records the action in the OMS. No software operates a switch.

## P2. Closing an outage

An outage is closed in the OMS only when every smart meter behind its failed device reports power. A crew
saying "done" is not enough: a second fault further down the line can keep homes dark after the first repair.

## P3. Critical customers

A critical customer (a hospital, a care home, a water pumping station, or a home on medical oxygen) hears its
restore time from a person on the desk. A text message may follow, but never replaces the call.

## P4. Restore times

The restore time sent to customers is the crew plan's estimate. If the outage lasts longer than that estimate,
customers get a new time before the old one passes.
